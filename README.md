FUTURE_DS_01 – Internship Data Science Project
Business Sales Performance Analytics
This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to identify sales and profit performance across categories, regions, top-selling products, and yearly trends.
Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
PivotTables
Charts
Data Analysis
Analysis
Sales and profit by category
Sales and profit by region
Revenue trend by year
Top 10 products by sales
Client-ready dashboard
Key Insights
Technology had the highest sales and profit among categories.
West had the highest sales and profit among regions.
Sales increased from 2015 to 2017, with 2017 showing the highest sales among the analyzed years.
The Top 10 Products analysis identifies the strongest-selling products.
Recommendations
Focus on Technology and continue supporting its strong performance.
Strengthen strategies in lower-performing regions.
Prioritize top-selling products with appropriate stock and marketing.
Investigate weaker categories and regions for improvement opportunities
